## The verdict (the standard FAST same-scores rule)
- **Core, decision 4:** `ref2tf` vs `ref2t` on the 210 (`tools/d4.py` on `evalsuite report --variant ref2tf --decode
  --ref ref2t`). Per set (math; IFEval strict, prompt level; HumanEval+ pass@1): the paired delta is above -5 points AND
  the exact two-sided McNemar p is at least 0.05. Judged on unrounded numbers.
- **RULER, the suite's line** (margins.json `ruler_net_items_lost_max` 1): `ref2tf` vs `ref2tf0` on the 24; net items lost
  (control-only right minus F1-only right) at most 1.
- **PASS:** all four lines pass. **FAIL:** a line fails (IFEval only after stage 2). **DIFFERS (HIGHER):** a set gains
  with p < 0.05. **VOID:** a step 0 gate fails.
- **Stage 2:** runs only if IFEval alone fails on the core 100 while math, HumanEval+ and RULER pass. `ref2tf` then
  decodes IFX's 220 stage-1 prompts, and IFEval's line becomes decision 4 on those 220 against `ref2t.r2`.
